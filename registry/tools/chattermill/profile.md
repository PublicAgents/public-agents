Chattermill is a customer-experience intelligence platform. Its [llms.txt](https://chattermill.com/llms.txt) (read 2026-11-18 by plain GET) opens "Chattermill (chattermill.com) is a customer experience intelligence platform that unifies feedback from every channel (surveys, support tickets, social media, app reviews and call transcripts) and uses AI to uncover why customers feel the way they do." Everything below is drawn from Chattermill's own marketing site, its help center and a keyless probe of its MCP endpoint, each read on 2026-11-18 and cited where it is used; this entry is filed by the registry's researcher from those public surfaces, not by Chattermill, so its provenance is third-party and it is unverified (the ownership file is absent, see Access and ownership).

## The agent surface

Chattermill runs a first-party, Chattermill-hosted MCP server. The [MCP server guide](https://docs.chattermill.com/en/articles/13943134-chattermill-mcp-server) (200, read 2026-11-18) says it "allows AI agents (such as Claude, Cursor, and Codex) to securely access your customer feedback", and the [MCP product page](https://chattermill.com/platform/mcp) (200, read 2026-11-18) frames it as "Plug verified customer intelligence straight into the AI agents you already use via MCP" that "Works with Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Glean, and any MCP-compatible agent."

The guide states the server "is fully hosted and managed by Chattermill" and names one endpoint per data region: EU `https://app.chattermill.com/mcp` and US `https://app-us.chattermill.com/mcp`. It says "These tools fall into two groups: Discovery tools and Data tools", and recommends setting them all to "Always Allow" because "these tools are read-only". The eight tools it documents:

- Discovery: `list_themes` — "Returns the categories and themes configured in a Chattermill project."; `list_attributes`; `search_attributes`; `search_observations` — "Searches clusters of related customer feedback using keywords or theme identifiers. Results include observation identifiers and representative snippets that can be used for further analysis."; `list_metric_options`.
- Data: `generate_highlights` — "Generates an AI-powered summary of customer feedback based on a query, in the exact same style you'd find in the Chattermill app."; `get_metrics` — "Returns structured quantitative data from Chattermill, such as NPS trends, sentiment metrics, or feedback volume over time."; `get_feedback` — "Retrieves individual customer feedback responses matching a query or filter. This allows AI agents to analyze verbatim feedback and validate insights using real customer comments."

## Why it claims cs.summarize-customer-feedback

The job asks that free-text customer feedback become themes with representative quotes and counts a leader can act on. Chattermill's MCP tools return exactly that on the agent surface, and the composition is Chattermill's own engine, not the connected model's: `generate_highlights` returns an AI summary of the feedback into key issues, trends or themes "in the exact same style you'd find in the Chattermill app"; `list_themes` returns the configured themes; `get_feedback` returns the verbatim comments behind them; and `get_metrics` returns the quantitative counts (volume, NPS, sentiment). The MCP product page puts the same shape in marketing terms: the FAQ answer to "How does the Chattermill MCP work?" lists that the agent can "Get quick summaries of customer feedback across any topic, segment or time period", "Access quantified insights, including issue volumes, metrics and customer sentiment", and "Pull raw verbatim feedback based on specific criteria"; and the page promises to "Trace every insight back to a real customer quote".

This is the first claim on `cs.summarize-customer-feedback`, a job created on 2026-11-18 (PR #382) as the customer-side analog of `hr.summarize-engagement-feedback`. Chattermill fits the customer side squarely: its llms.txt describes a platform built for "CX leaders, Voice of Customer managers, product managers", unifying "surveys, support tickets, social media, app reviews and call transcripts". The vendor-computed-composition shape here is the same one that let Thematic claim the employee analog, and the opposite of an external-composer seam where the model, not the tool, does the summarizing.

Two honest limits. The tools are feedback-source agnostic: they operate on whatever feedback an organization has loaded into its Chattermill projects, and I have not observed customer feedback flowing through the MCP, which would require a logged-in account with such data. The claim rests on the documented tool semantics and Chattermill's own marketing, both the vendor's words. And it is not a measurement: no third-party figure for theme accuracy (the job's "theme accuracy rated by the team"), share of feedback mapped to a theme, or "time to report" was found, so those measures stay empty. The MCP product page's "Trace every insight back to a real customer quote - no hallucinations" is a vendor claim, recorded as a claim, not a verified finding.

## Access and ownership

The MCP is not keyless. A keyless POST `initialize` to `https://app.chattermill.com/mcp` returned HTTP 401 on 2026-11-18 with `WWW-Authenticate: Bearer resource_metadata="https://app.chattermill.com/.well-known/oauth-protected-resource/mcp", scope="openid profile email offline_access mcp:read"` and a body of `{"error":"Unauthorized"}`; the US endpoint `https://app-us.chattermill.com/mcp` answered identically with its own regional metadata URL. The guide documents two auth methods: OAuth (recommended), where "OAuth allows users to securely connect their AI agent to Chattermill using their existing Chattermill account" with login at `auth.chattermill.com`, and a bearer token using a Chattermill API key, which the guide notes "is currently restricted to Chattermill admins". Access runs under the user's own Chattermill roles and permissions.

Ownership is unverified. `https://chattermill.com/.well-known/public-agents.json` returned 404 on 2026-11-18, so no file names this slug; this entry stays third-party and unverified until the domain acknowledges it.
