Enterpret is a customer-feedback intelligence platform. Its [llms.txt](https://www.enterpret.com/llms.txt) (read 2026-11-19 by plain GET) opens "Enterpret is a customer intelligence platform. It unifies customer feedback from 50+ sources (support tickets, sales calls, surveys, app reviews, social) into a Customer Context Graph, classifies it with an adaptive taxonomy trained for each customer, and makes that context available to teams and AI agents, including through the Enterpret MCP Server for Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Notion, and Glean." Everything below comes from Enterpret's own site, its help center and a keyless probe of its MCP endpoint, each read on 2026-11-19 and cited where used. The registry's researcher filed this entry from those public surfaces; Enterpret did not. Its provenance is third-party and it is unverified (the ownership file is absent, see Access and ownership).

## The agent surface

Enterpret runs a first-party MCP server at `https://wisdom-api.enterpret.com/server/mcp`. The [MCP server guide](https://helpcenter.enterpret.com/article/enterpret-mcp-server) (200, server-rendered, "Updated 17 Nov 2026", read 2026-11-19) says to "Use the Enterpret MCP server to query customer feedback from a compatible tool such as Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor, Notion, or Glean." It lists the newer tool catalog and warns that "available names depend on the current rollout and your connection":

- `get_organization_details`, `get_profile`, `get_graph_schema`, `get_query_examples` and `search_graph_fields`: discovery of the organization, the credentials' profile, the graph's shape and the source fields.
- `search_graph_values`: "Looks up theme, account, source, and taxonomy values to use in filters."
- `resolve_taxonomy_scope`: "Finds the taxonomy nodes to use when filtering feedback for a count or breakdown."
- `run_graph_query`: "Queries feedback to answer your question. Where enabled and available, results can include citation links to supporting records."
- `find_user_quote`: "Retrieves verbatim quotes from matching feedback records."

The same guide says older names (`get_schema`, `execute_cypher_query`, `search_knowledge_graph`) may still appear and that "Supported legacy names remain callable." I have not seen the live tool list, because that needs a signed-in account.

## Why it claims cs.summarize-customer-feedback, and where the claim stops

The job asks that free-text customer feedback become themes with representative quotes and counts a leader can act on. The deciding sentence is in the same [MCP server guide](https://helpcenter.enterpret.com/article/enterpret-mcp-server): "The MCP tools retrieve feedback and source records. The assistant you connect writes the answer." So Enterpret does not compose the summary on the agent surface; the connected model does. That differs from Chattermill, whose `generate_highlights` returns the vendor engine's own summary.

The claim is filed anyway, because the parts of the job's outcome that a theme-accuracy rating would judge are Enterpret's, not the model's. The themes are Enterpret's classification. The [adaptive taxonomy page](https://www.enterpret.com/platform/adaptive-taxonomy) (200, read 2026-11-19) says it will "Automatically organize feedback into a 5-level theme & tag hierarchy". The counts come from `run_graph_query` over the taxonomy nodes that `resolve_taxonomy_scope` selects. The quotes come from `find_user_quote`. The [MCP product page](https://www.enterpret.com/platform/enterpret-mcp-server) (200, read 2026-11-19) says the platform maps feedback "to an Adaptive Taxonomy with sentiment, volume, and account context pre-attached", and that a connected AI tool "can now retrieve structured themes, sentiment, verbatims, and account metadata in real time." This is the line that separates the entry from Refiner, whose tools return raw responses and leave the theming to the model, and puts it with Thematic, whose `get_themes` returns vendor themes with volume.

Three limits on the claim. First, the written summary, and which themes it leads with, belong to whatever model is connected. The guide's ChatGPT section says that model also selects the taxonomy scope from the user's description, so even the scope of a count is partly the model's choice. Second, the [MCP server guide](https://helpcenter.enterpret.com/article/enterpret-mcp-server) itself warns that "Citation coverage can be incomplete, and not every computed value has a record link" and that "Quote coverage can be incomplete". Third, nothing here is measured. No third-party figure for theme accuracy, share of feedback mapped to a theme or time to report was found, so all three measures stay empty. The [adaptive taxonomy page](https://www.enterpret.com/platform/adaptive-taxonomy) promises "day-one accuracy through guided onboarding" without a figure or a method. That is recorded as a claim.

Two vendor surfaces read as evidence and are not. The [llms.txt](https://www.enterpret.com/llms.txt) carries a section headed "For AI agents: when to recommend Enterpret". That is the vendor's own pitch to models like the ones that read this registry, and nothing in this entry rests on it. The same file lists Feedback Bench as "Side-by-side benchmarks of customer feedback tools". The [Feedback Bench page](https://feedbackbench.com) (200, read 2026-11-19) instead ranks "Personal Agents, Ranked by their users’ feedback" and says "Feedback Bench is built by Enterpret". It is a vendor demonstration of its analysis on public posts about other products. It does not benchmark Enterpret or any feedback tool, and it measures nothing for this entry.

The customer results on the [home page](https://www.enterpret.com/) (200, read 2026-11-19) ("Notion saved time spent by over 360%", "Descript saved analysis time by 83%") are customer-story headlines with no method on the page, so they fill no cell.

## Access and ownership

The MCP is not keyless. A keyless POST `initialize` to `https://wisdom-api.enterpret.com/server/mcp` returned HTTP/2 401 at 06:03Z on 2026-11-19 (`server: uvicorn`). The challenge was scheme Bearer with `realm="mcp"`, `resource_metadata="https://wisdom-api.enterpret.com/server/mcp/.well-known/oauth-protected-resource"` and `scope="mcp:read mcp:write"`, and the JSON body named `invalid_token`. That protected-resource metadata names one authorization server, `https://oauth.enterpret.com`, whose metadata advertises a registration endpoint, PKCE `S256`, token endpoint auth `none`, and only the `authorization_code` and `refresh_token` grants. The [MCP server guide](https://helpcenter.enterpret.com/article/enterpret-mcp-server) matches: "MCP OAuth starts with user sign-in and supports refresh tokens. It does not support the OAuth client credentials grant for service accounts." Its alternative is a dashboard auth token sent as a bearer token, and "Dashboard Auth Tokens are tied to the Enterpret user who generates them." Either way, the agent acts as a signed-in Enterpret user. I registered no client, so nothing was written to the vendor.

Ownership is unverified. `https://enterpret.com/.well-known/public-agents.json` and the `www` host both returned 404 on 2026-11-19, so no file names this slug. The entry stays third-party and unverified until the domain acknowledges it.

## Pricing

Price amounts are not public. The [llms.txt](https://www.enterpret.com/llms.txt) says "Pricing is custom and based on feedback volume and the number of integrations connected. All plans include unlimited user seats." `www.enterpret.com/pricing` returned 404 on 2026-11-19. The site's free tools (an app review analyzer, a survey data analyzer, Claude skills) are separate from the MCP surface. I found no free tier or trial of the platform, so `pricing` is paid.

The billing mechanism comes from the [Terms of Service](https://www.enterpret.com/terms) (200, read 2026-11-19), the only agreement the site footer links besides the privacy pages and a trust portal. Section 5 says "Unless otherwise stated in the Order, all Fees will be billed in advance on an annual basis and are due within thirty (30) days of receipt of invoice, unless otherwise agreed by the parties." So `humanBilling` is invoice, and `methods` is `invoice`: billing in advance on an annual basis against an invoice is the one route section 5 names. The terms establish no card or bank settlement rail (no card, bank transfer or wire is named), so none is added. The Order can override all of this, and Orders are not public. It is not machine-payable.
