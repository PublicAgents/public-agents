Unwrap (Unwrap.ai) is a customer-feedback intelligence platform. Its [llms.txt](https://www.unwrap.ai/llms.txt) (read 2026-11-19 by plain GET) says it "aggregates customer feedback from every channel (support tickets, surveys, app reviews, sales calls), uses custom NLP to auto-tag and categorize it, and surfaces actionable product insights without manual analysis." Everything below comes from Unwrap's own site, its knowledge base and a keyless probe of its MCP endpoint, each read on 2026-11-19 and cited where used. The registry's researcher filed this entry from those public surfaces; Unwrap did not. Its provenance is third-party and it is unverified (the ownership file is absent, see Access and ownership).

## The agent surface

Unwrap runs a first-party MCP server at `https://nlp.api.production.unwrap.ai/mcp`. The [Unwrap MCP article](https://docs.unwrap.ai/articles/6412771011-unwrap-mcp) (200, server-rendered, "Last updated 1 month ago" as of 2026-11-19) says "The Unwrap MCP gives you access to the same tools that power the Unwrap Assistant, allowing you to get the same great Assistant experience natively in your AI model of choice." Its tool table lists sixteen tools. The ones that carry the job:

- `search_groups`: "Semantically searches taxonomy groups to find issues or themes relevant to a query."
- `search_actionable_groups`: "Returns the most actionable groups for a question, with optional filtering and numeric segment sorting."
- `plot_chart`: "Generates charts or tables over time or by breakdown for trends, rankings, share comparisons, and other visual analyses."
- `sample_feedback_entries`: "Creates a named sample of feedback entries, either as a representative random sample or a targeted semantic sample."
- `analyze_entry_text`: "Runs a custom instruction or question across all entries in a saved sample and returns prevalence statistics plus example results."
- `classify_feedback_entries`: "Classifies entries in a saved sample into predefined labels and creates sub-samples for each resulting label."

The rest are discovery and help: `get_started`, `list_views`, `list_feedback_integrations`, `search_segments`, `list_segment_field_values`, `discover_filter_schema`, `query_feedback_tool`, `search_documentation`, `read_documentation_article` and `leave_feedback`. The same article says "All Unwrap tools are currently read-only." Two details on the vendor's own pages do not sit easily with that. `leave_feedback` is described as "Leave feedback on the MCP directly to the Unwrap team", which sends something to the vendor. And the Claude setup steps tell the admin to set "Write/Delete tools" to need approval, though no such tool is listed. The [announcement post](https://www.unwrap.ai/post/unwrap-mcp) (200, read 2026-11-19) says the MCP gives "twelve tools spanning the full feedback intelligence stack" and that "All twelve tools exposed by the Unwrap MCP are read-only." The knowledge base lists sixteen today, so the post's count is older than the table. The post also carries two dates, August 28, 2026 under the author line and May 19, 2026 beside it; which one is the publication date is not stated. I have not seen the live tool list, because that needs a signed-in account.

## Why it claims cs.summarize-customer-feedback, and where the claim stops

The job asks that free-text customer feedback become themes with representative quotes and counts a leader can act on. On Unwrap's surface the themes are its own taxonomy Groups, returned by `search_groups` and `search_actionable_groups`; the counts and trends come from `plot_chart`; the examples come from `sample_feedback_entries`. The analysis is also Unwrap's, not only the connected model's. The [MCP security overview](https://docs.unwrap.ai/articles/5132579970-unwrap-mcp-server-security-overview-faq) (200, "Last updated 2 months ago" as of 2026-11-19) says that "Tools that perform semantic analysis", for example "classifying or summarizing sampled Entries", "invoke an LLM server-side." It lists what a user can retrieve, including "Feedback Groups and taxonomy structure", feedback summaries that are "condensed representations of Entries rather than raw source text", and "Metrics" such as "feedback volume, favorability, relative share, resolution rate". That places Unwrap with Chattermill and Thematic, where the vendor computes the themes and counts, rather than with Refiner, whose tools return raw responses for the model to theme.

Three limits on the claim. First, the final written answer is the connected model's: the [Unwrap MCP article](https://docs.unwrap.ai/articles/6412771011-unwrap-mcp) says the client "will be able to use the Unwrap tools to explore your teams feedback, and give you a data backed answer." Second, the feedback summaries the tools return are vendor text: the [MCP security overview](https://docs.unwrap.ai/articles/5132579970-unwrap-mcp-server-security-overview-faq) calls them "condensed representations of Entries rather than raw source text", so a representative quote taken from this surface may be a paraphrase, not the customer's words. Third, nothing here is measured. No third-party figure for theme accuracy, share of feedback mapped to a theme or time to report was found, so all three measures stay empty.

Unwrap's accuracy figures disagree with each other and carry no method. The [llms.txt](https://www.unwrap.ai/llms.txt) cites "94% auto-tagging accuracy" from the Why Unwrap page. The [Why Unwrap page](https://www.unwrap.ai/why-unwrap) (200, read 2026-11-19) shows "90%+ Accuracy" over "Average precision for feedback tagging", and claims "Best-in class accuracy, as verified by external parties." It names no external party, and "94%" does not appear on it. These are the vendor's claims and fill no cell.

## Access and ownership

The MCP is not keyless. A keyless POST `initialize` to `https://nlp.api.production.unwrap.ai/mcp` returned HTTP/2 401 at 12:05Z on 2026-11-19 (`server: uvicorn`, body `{"error":"Unauthorized"}`). The challenge was scheme Bearer with `realm="mcp"` and `resource_metadata="https://nlp.api.production.unwrap.ai/.well-known/oauth-protected-resource/mcp"`. That metadata names the same host as the authorization server and lists seven scopes, all read scopes (for example `team:*:groups:read`, `team:*:entries:read`, `team:*:charts:read`). The authorization server metadata advertises a registration endpoint, client ID metadata documents, PKCE `S256`, token endpoint auth `client_secret_basic` and `none`, and the grants `authorization_code`, `client_credentials` and `refresh_token`.

The [MCP security overview](https://docs.unwrap.ai/articles/5132579970-unwrap-mcp-server-security-overview-faq) describes two ways in. Individual users connect through dynamic client registration and act with their own permissions. An org admin can instead create a static OAuth client: "Machine-to-machine access is supported via client_credentials." Those tokens "carry organization-scoped access rather than an individual user's scope." So an agent can hold its own credential, but only one an Unwrap admin created. The overview also says "All OAuth tokens are currently read-only and enforced server-side", "Every MCP tool requires an explanation parameter", and "There is no self-hosted or on-premise option." I registered no client, so nothing was written to the vendor.

Unwrap also has a GraphQL API. The [API overview](https://docs.unwrap.ai/articles/4212347322-api%2Fapi-overview) (200, read 2026-11-19) says it lets developers "Pull feedback entries, their full conversations and the Groups each entry belongs to" and "Pull your taxonomy", and the [usage restrictions](https://docs.unwrap.ai/articles/4888229887-api%2Fusage-restrictions) say "API requests are limited to 100 requests per token, per minute." Unlike the MCP, the API also writes: it uploads entries, builds Groups and deletes entries.

Ownership is unverified. `https://unwrap.ai/.well-known/public-agents.json` and the `www` host both returned 404 on 2026-11-19, so no file names this slug. The entry stays third-party and unverified until the domain acknowledges it.

## Pricing

The [pricing page](https://www.unwrap.ai/pricing) (200, read 2026-11-19) says "Starting at $24,000 per year" and "With options to start with a 30-day trial based on your data." Packages "depend on your team's monthly feedback volume and the integrations you plug into Unwrap", and "You'll never be charged by seat". "API access" is listed among the features. A 30-day trial is not a free tier, so `pricing` is paid.

The billing mechanism comes from the [Terms of Use](https://www.unwrap.ai/legal/terms-of-service) (200, read 2026-11-19), which say "these Terms are effective as of November 18, 2026" for new customers. Under section 7, Order Form fees "shall be invoiced in advance in accordance with the billing frequency stated therein", the customer pays "any fees associated with an electronic funds transfer (i.e., ACH, wire transfer)", and a card is billed "In certain instances where your payment method is via credit card". So `humanBilling` is invoice and `methods` are bank transfer and card. The Order Form can set payment forms and terms, and Order Forms are not public. It is not machine-payable.
